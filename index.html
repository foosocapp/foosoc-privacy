Privacy Policy for Foosoc App

Last Updated: January 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Foosoc ("we", "our", or "us") is an independently developed football match tracking mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how Foosoc collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 User-Provided Information

  • Player Information: Player names, ages, positions, and optional photos entered by the user.
  • Match Data: Team names, scores, match duration, goals, assists, cards, substitutions, and other in-match events.
  • Team Information: Team names, formations, and lineup assignments.

2.2 Device Permissions

  • Storage Access: Used only when you choose to select player photos from your device.
  • Camera Access: Used only if you choose to capture player photos within the app.

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Advertising ID: Used by Google AdMob for advertising and ad measurement.
  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, and basic diagnostics.
  • Crash & Performance Data: Anonymous crash logs to improve app stability.

3. How We Use Information

  • Enable core match tracking and team management features.
  • Store match history locally on your device.
  • Improve app performance and reliability.
  • Display advertisements via Google AdMob.

4. Data Storage & Security

4.1 Local Storage

  • All match, team, and player data is stored locally on your device.
  • Player photos are stored locally and never uploaded to Foosoc servers.

4.2 Data Retention

  • Data remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data.

4.3 Security

  • We use reasonable technical safeguards to protect local data.
  • No personal data is transmitted to Foosoc servers.

5. Data Sharing

5.1 We Do Not Sell Data

Foosoc does not sell, rent, or trade user data.

5.2 Third-Party Services

Foosoc integrates with the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display ads. AdMob may collect Advertising ID, device information, and ad interaction data in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.

5.3 Legal Requirements

Information may be disclosed if required by law or legal process.

6. Advertising & Analytics

6.1 Advertising

Foosoc uses Google AdMob to serve ads. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on user consent and region.

6.2 User Controls

  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID via device settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads in Android settings.

7. User Rights & Controls

  • Edit or delete player and match data within the app.
  • Remove player photos at any time.
  • Clear all data by uninstalling the app.

8. GDPR & EEA Users

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under GDPR including:

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to request deletion
  • Right to object or restrict processing

User consent is requested where required for personalized advertising. Consent can be withdrawn at any time via device or app settings.

9. Children’s Privacy

Foosoc is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. International Data Transfers

Foosoc does not transfer user data to external servers. Any international data handling is performed solely by Google AdMob according to Google’s policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Updates will be reflected by the “Last Updated” date.
