Privacy Policy for Foosoc App

Last Updated: January 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Foosoc ("we", "our", or "us") is an independently developed football match tracking mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the Foosoc app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 User-Provided Information

  • Player Information: Player names, positions, ages, and optional photos entered voluntarily by the user.
  • Match Data: Team names, scores, match duration, goals, assists, cards, substitutions, and related events.
  • Team Information: Team names, formations, and lineups.

2.2 Device Permissions

  • Storage Access: Used only when the user selects player photos from the device.
  • Camera Access: Used only if the user chooses to take photos within the app.

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Advertising ID: Collected by Google AdMob for ad serving and measurement.
  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, and basic diagnostics.
  • Crash & Performance Data: Anonymous crash logs to improve app stability.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide core match tracking and team management features.
  • Store match history locally on the device.
  • Improve performance, reliability, and usability.
  • Display advertisements through Google AdMob.

4. Data Storage & Security

4.1 Local Storage

  • All match, team, and player data is stored locally on the user’s device.
  • Player photos are stored locally and are never uploaded to Foosoc servers.

4.2 Data Retention

  • Data remains on the device until the app is uninstalled or app data is cleared.

4.3 Security

  • Reasonable technical measures are used to protect locally stored data.
  • No personal data is transmitted to Foosoc-operated servers.

5. Data Sharing

5.1 No Sale of Data

Foosoc does not sell, rent, or trade user data.

5.2 Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: Used to display ads. AdMob may collect Advertising ID, device information, and ad interaction data in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.

5.3 Legal Disclosure

Information may be disclosed if required by law or legal process.

6. Advertising & Analytics

6.1 Advertising

Foosoc uses Google AdMob to serve ads. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on user consent, region, and Google Families Policy requirements.

6.2 User Controls

  • Users can reset or delete their Advertising ID via device settings.
  • Users can opt out of personalized ads through Android system settings.

7. User Controls & Data Management

  • Users can edit or delete player and match data within the app.
  • Player photos can be removed or changed at any time.
  • All data can be removed by uninstalling the app.

8. GDPR & EEA Users

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under GDPR, including the right to access, delete, or restrict processing of your data.

Consent for personalized advertising is requested where required and can be withdrawn at any time via device or app settings.

9. Children & Families Compliance

Foosoc is suitable for users of all ages and is rated 3+ on Google Play. The app does not require account creation and does not knowingly collect real-world identity information.

All player names, photos, and match details are entered voluntarily by the user and are stored locally on the device. Foosoc does not encourage the entry of real personal information.

Advertising shown in the app is served by Google AdMob and complies with Google Play Families Policy, including the use of appropriate ad content and, where applicable, non-personalized advertising.

10. International Data Transfers

Foosoc does not transfer user data to external servers. Any international data handling is performed solely by Google AdMob in accordance with Google’s privacy and data protection policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Changes will be reflected by updating the “Last Updated” date.
