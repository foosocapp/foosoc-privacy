Privacy Policy for Foosoc App

Last Updated: October 20, 2025

1. Introduction

Foosoc ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application ("Foosoc" or "the App") collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you use our soccer match tracking application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • Player Information: We collect and store player names, ages, and optional photos that you voluntarily provide when creating teams and matches
  • Match Data: We store match information including team names, scores, player events (goals, assists, cards, substitutions), and match timing
  • Team Information: Team names, formations, and player assignments

2.2 Device Information

  • Device Storage: The app requires access to device storage only when you choose to add player photos from your gallery
  • Camera Access: Only if you choose to take photos directly within the app for player profiles

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: We may collect information about how you use the App, such as feature usage frequency and match creation patterns
  • Crash Reports: Anonymous crash data and performance metrics to improve app stability

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Core Functionality: To enable match tracking, player statistics, and team management
  • Data Persistence: To save your match data locally on your device and allow match resumption
  • App Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve user experience
  • Technical Support: To troubleshoot issues and provide customer support

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

  • All match data, player information, and team details are stored locally on your device using SQLite database
  • Player photos are stored locally and are not transmitted to external servers
  • Match history and statistics remain on your device unless explicitly shared by you

4.2 Data Retention

  • Your data persists on your device until you uninstall the app or manually clear data
  • You can export match results and statistics via the share functionality

4.3 Security

  • We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data on the device
  • Local database encryption is used where supported by the device
  • No sensitive personal data is transmitted to external servers without your consent

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

5.2 Limited Sharing

We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With Your Consent: When you choose to share match results or statistics through device sharing features
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our legal rights

5.3 Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or social media platforms that would collect your personal data.

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Access and Control

  • You can view, edit, or delete player information at any time within the App
  • Match data can be cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing app data
  • You can choose not to add player photos or use real names

6.2 Data Export

  • You can export match results and statistics using the built-in share functionality
  • Screenshots of match results can be saved to your device or shared directly

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. International Data Transfers

All data is stored locally on your device and is not transferred internationally unless you explicitly share match results across different regions.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the App and updating the "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

11. Additional Provisions

11.1 Photo Usage

  • Player photos are used solely for identification within the App
  • Photos are stored locally and are not used for any other purpose
  • You can remove or change player photos at any time

11.2 Match Data Usage

  • Match statistics are used only for historical tracking and in-app display
  • No match data is used for commercial purposes or shared with third parties

11.3 Offline Functionality

The App is designed to work completely offline. No internet connection is required for core functionality, ensuring your data remains private and secure on your device.
